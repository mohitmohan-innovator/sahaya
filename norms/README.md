# Knowledge corpus — download these yourself (not redistributed here)

Place the ORIGINAL PDFs in this folder before running build_index.py:

1. **Items and Norms of Assistance from SDRF and NDRF** — Ministry of Home
   Affairs, Government of India. Get the latest revision from
   ndmindia.mha.gov.in (search "SDRF norms items and norms of assistance").
2. **Kerala Government Orders on relief assistance** — 2 to 4 G.O.s from the
   Revenue / Disaster Management department, via sdma.kerala.gov.in and the
   Kerala G.O. portal. Orders relating to the 2024 Wayanad landslides and
   CMDRF top-up assistance are ideal.

Why originals: SAHAYA's Norms agent must cite real clauses from real
documents. Never paraphrase these into your own files and never hardcode a
rupee amount anywhere in code or prompts — entitlements are retrieved and
cited, or the agent answers "indeterminate".
